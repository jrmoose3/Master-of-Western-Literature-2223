Coursework for the Master's of Western Literature was often a mixture of written tests, weekly assignments, and research papers. Some of the more major assignments are available for view in this Overview folder.
<br>
<br>

2. Jacob Moose Final Essay (Highlights of European Literature): A comparative research paper on the theme of metamorphosis.<br>
3. Jacob Moose_Masterthesis: Final thesis submitted for the completeion of Master degree. Title, "Towards A  Re-Reading Of Sexual Imagery in the Poetry of Anthony Hecht." <br>
4. Postmodernism_Final Presentation: The written text for an oral presentation given in English Literature Topics in Post-45 American Literature course.
